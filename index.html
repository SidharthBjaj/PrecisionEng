At Precision Engineering Ltd, we bring over a decade of experience delivering high-impact civil projects across British Columbia. We are known for cost-effective solutions that meet strict BC regulatory standards.

Our team of P.Eng licensed engineers brings meticulous attention to detail to every project — from feasibility studies and site servicing plans through to construction-phase engineering and final record drawings.

What We Do

Core Engineering Services

01

Civil Engineering

  • Site servicing and grading plans
  • Stormwater management plans (SWMPs)
  • Watermain, storm, and sanitary sewer design
  • Roadworks and street infrastructure
  • Pump station design
  • Erosion and sediment control (ESC) plans
02

Feasibility Studies

Assessing development potential through lot layouts, servicing concepts, and proformas. Our work adheres to Municipal Engineering Design Criteria, incorporating sustainable systems such as bioswales, rain gardens, detention tanks, and exfiltration infrastructure.

03

Permitting & Approvals

  • Servicing Agreements
  • ESC, fill, and building permits
  • Municipal development documentation
  • Direct liaison with approving authorities to streamline timelines
04

Cost Estimates & Proformas

Detailed cost breakdowns, material quantity take-offs, and development proformas supporting budgeting, financing, and project viability assessments across residential, commercial, and mixed-use developments.

05

Project Coordination

Collaboration with architects, planners, contractors, and municipal staff throughout the project lifecycle — supporting RFIs, tendering, inspection, record drawing review, and construction-phase engineering services.

06

Project Delivery

Carrying projects from conceptual stage through design into construction. Includes ESC monitoring, field reviews, and comprehensive record drawing reviews to ensure regulatory compliance at every phase.
